Marked Up

Find your own vehicle by its plate.

Only vehicles their owners enrolled here can be found. One position per vehicle is kept, and it is deleted the moment sharing stops.

Enrol a vehicle you own

Open this tab on the phone that rides in the vehicle and tap Share location. Keep that page open with the screen on. The keys for your vehicles live only in this browser, so use Copy phone link to move them to another phone.

Standard-library Python on the server, plain JavaScript in the browser. No accounts, no names, no history.
